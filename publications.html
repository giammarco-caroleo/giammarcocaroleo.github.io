    • Maric, A., Caroleo, G., Albini, A., Maiolino, P., Calinon, S. — “Diffusion-based Inverse Model of a Distributed Tactile Sensor for Object Pose Estimation”

      Currently on arXiv.

    • Caroleo, G., Albini, A., De Martini, D., Barfoot, T.D., Maiolino, P. — “Tiny LiDARs for Manipulator Self-Awareness: Sensor Characterization and Initial Localization Experiments”

      IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS 2025).

      This work shows how miniaturized, low-cost Time-of-Flight sensors mounted on manipulators provide enough information to localize objects in manipulators surroundings. A preliminary sensor characterization is presented along with a probabilistic sensor model.

      Paper link

    • Sander*, J., Caroleo, G.*, Albini, A., Maiolino, P. — “Estimating Scene Flow in Robot Surroundings with Distributed Miniaturized Time-of-Flight Sensors”

      2025 34th IEEE International Conference on Robot and Human Interactive Communication (RO-MAN), (pp. 493-499).

      We exploit distributed miniaturized Time-of-Flight sensors to estimate scene flow around robots, enabling fast motion understanding in dynamic environments and paving the way for responsive, motion-aware behaviors.

      Paper link

    • Zhong, S., Albini, A., Caroleo, G., Maiolino, P. — “Improving Tactile Gesture Recognition with Optical Flow”

      2025 34th IEEE International Conference on Robot and Human Interactive Communication (RO-MAN), (pp. 2246-2252).

      Applying optical-flow to tactile images, this paper boosts gesture recognition accuracy by turning spatiotemporal tactile patterns into motion-like features — a compact trick with big practical gains for touch-based interfaces.

      Paper link

    • Caroleo, G., Albini, A., Maiolino, P. — “A Cross-Task Visuo–Tactile Representation using Point Clouds”

      Intelligent Service Robotics (2025), pp. 1-13.

      We present an augmented point-cloud representation that fuses vision and physical properties and can be used across tasks. A simple yet effective way to combine visual and tactile information in an interpretable unified representation.

      Paper link

    • Caroleo, G., Albini, A., Maiolino, P. — “Soft Robot Localization Using Distributed Miniaturized Time-of-Flight Sensors”
